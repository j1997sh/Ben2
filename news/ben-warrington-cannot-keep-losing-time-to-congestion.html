WarringtonCampaign update

The campaign focuses on practical road and public-transport improvements.

Ben’s campaign is using local visits and conversations to understand how regional mayoral powers could make a practical difference in Warrington. The focus is on the problems residents and employers encounter every day, and on where coordination across councils and services can help.

What Ben is hearing

People are raising a mixture of immediate local concerns and longer-term questions about growth, transport, public services and opportunity. The campaign’s approach is to connect those local concerns to decisions the new mayor could realistically influence.

What happens next

Feedback from Warrington will continue to feed into Ben’s regional plan and the local campaigns highlighted on this site. Residents can also use the local area hub to see related events and ways to get involved.
