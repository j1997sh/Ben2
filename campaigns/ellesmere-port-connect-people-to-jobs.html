Connect people to jobs

Improve buses, roads and regional links to employment sites.

Why this matters in Ellesmere Port

Improve buses, roads and regional links to employment sites. The mayor will not control every local service directly, but the role can coordinate investment, convene partners and put regional weight behind problems that cross organisational boundaries.

What Ben is asking for

A practical commitment to make this issue part of the mayoral programme, measure progress and keep local residents updated on what is changing.

What local evidence tells us

Local conversations point to a consistent theme: people want visible progress on the things that affect everyday life, not another layer of process. This campaign gives residents a way to back that priority and add their own experience.
