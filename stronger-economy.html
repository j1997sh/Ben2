A stronger economy

Back employers, investment and local growth — with skills and infrastructure that match ambition.

Growth should be something people can see and feel.

Cheshire & Warrington has huge economic strengths, from advanced manufacturing and life sciences to logistics, energy, professional services and a strong network of smaller businesses.

The mayor should make it easier to invest, recruit and grow here — and make sure local people can access the jobs and opportunities that growth creates.

01

Back local employers and investment

The mayor should be a visible champion for the region, working with employers to remove barriers to investment and making a stronger case nationally and internationally for Cheshire & Warrington.

02

Connect skills to real opportunities

Skills policy should respond to the jobs employers actually need to fill. Colleges, training providers and businesses should have a clearer regional partnership around apprenticeships, retraining and technical skills.

03

Make growth easier to deliver

Transport, housing, planning and infrastructure all affect whether businesses can invest. The mayor can bring those decisions together and help unlock sites and projects that individual organisations cannot deliver alone.
