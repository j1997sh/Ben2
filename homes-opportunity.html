Homes & opportunity

Build the right homes in the right places, with the transport and infrastructure communities need.

New homes need to come with a plan for the places around them.

Cheshire & Warrington needs homes people can afford and communities can support. But growth loses public confidence when housing is planned separately from roads, schools, health services, public transport and town centres.

The mayor should help coordinate housing and infrastructure across boundaries, unlock regeneration and make better use of brownfield land while respecting the character of local places.

01

Plan homes alongside infrastructure

Housing numbers should not sit in isolation. New development needs credible plans for transport, schools, health services, utilities and community facilities from the outset.

02

Unlock regeneration and brownfield opportunity

The mayor can help assemble sites, coordinate investment and bring partners together to regenerate land that is difficult for individual councils or developers to unlock alone.

03

Keep local character in the plan

Regional coordination should not mean identical development everywhere. Local identity, design quality and the needs of existing communities should remain central to decisions about growth.
