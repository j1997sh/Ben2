Better transport

Reliable buses, stronger rail links, safer roads and better connections to jobs and services.

Transport should make everyday life easier.

For too many people, getting around Cheshire & Warrington is harder than it should be. Buses do not always connect where people need to go, bottlenecks waste time and poor links can hold back access to jobs, education and services.

The new mayor should use the powers of the role to join up transport decisions across the region and focus investment on the journeys people actually make.

01

Make buses more useful and reliable

A better bus network should be built around real journeys: getting to work, college, health appointments and town centres. The mayor can use regional coordination to improve routes, frequency and connections rather than leaving every service to operate in isolation.

02

Tackle the bottlenecks people notice every day

Road investment should concentrate on the junctions, pinch points and corridors that cost residents and businesses time. The goal is not simply more road building, but targeted improvements that keep people and goods moving.

03

Strengthen rail and cross-region connections

Cheshire & Warrington needs stronger links between its towns and to the wider North West. The mayor should be a clear regional voice for better rail services, stations and interchange between rail, bus, walking and cycling.
