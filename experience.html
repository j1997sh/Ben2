Experience

Leadership is about delivery — bringing people together, making decisions and turning plans into results.

Experience that can be put to work

Four parts of Ben’s experience, each tied directly to what the new mayor needs to deliver.

Getting projects moving

Working across organisations and priorities to turn decisions into delivery — with a focus on practical outcomes rather than process for its own sake.

Understanding growth

Seeing infrastructure, skills and investment through the lens of employers and communities — and understanding what helps places attract investment and create opportunity.

Listening locally

Keeping local places and identities central to regional decision-making, so the mayoralty strengthens communities rather than feeling like another distant layer.

Building coalitions

Bringing councils, businesses, public services and communities together around shared outcomes — because the biggest regional challenges cannot be solved by one organisation alone.

Experience only matters if it delivers.

The campaign connects Ben’s background directly to the priorities of the new mayoralty.
