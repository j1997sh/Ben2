About Ben

Local leadership, practical ambition and experience that can be put to work for Cheshire & Warrington.

A mayor focused on getting things done.

The first mayoralty is an opportunity to bring Cheshire and Warrington together around the issues people encounter every day: transport, jobs, safer communities and places that are able to grow well.

Ben’s campaign is built around practical leadership, local identity and a clear test: whether the new mayor actually delivers for local people.

Why I’m standing

“The new mayoralty should be judged by what it actually delivers for local people.”

The job should not be about creating another distant tier of politics. It should be about using new powers to solve problems that cross council boundaries and unlock opportunities individual places cannot deliver alone.

Experience that can be put to work

Ben’s experience is not a separate story from the campaign. It is part of the case for why he can make the new mayoralty work from day one.

That means understanding delivery, growth, local communities and the coalitions needed to get councils, employers and public services moving in the same direction.

Local focus, regional ambition

Cheshire & Warrington is not one place. The mayor needs to understand the different identities, economies and priorities across the region while using the role to solve problems that cross local boundaries.

The campaign will keep that balance at its heart: local listening, practical regional leadership and a focus on outcomes.

A practical campaign for strong leadership, better transport, safer communities and a thriving regional economy.

Ben
